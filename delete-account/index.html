Delete your Card Due Tracker account

Card Due Tracker by Anvaya Technologies Inc.

Card Due Tracker keeps your account and all its data on your phone only. Anvaya Technologies Inc. does not hold any of your data on servers, so deleting it on the phone removes it completely.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Card Due Tracker and unlock it.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Under Signed in, tap Delete account and confirm.

This permanently deletes your profile, cards, reminders, payment history, settings and connected email accounts, and asks Google to cancel the app's Gmail access.

If you no longer have the app

  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from that phone.
  • To cancel Gmail access, go to myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party apps and services, choose Card Due Tracker and remove access.
  • Backup files you saved yourself are yours to delete wherever you stored them.

Need help?

Email support@anvayatech.ca with the subject "Delete my account". We don't hold your data, but we'll help you with the steps. No data is kept after deletion.
